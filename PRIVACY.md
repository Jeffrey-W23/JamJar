# JamJar Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 12 October 2026

JamJar ("the Bot") is a Discord bot that generates random game themes, genres and limitations for game developers and game jam participants. This policy explains what information the Bot handles.

## What the Bot collects

**The Bot does not collect, store or sell personal data.**

When you use a slash command (for example `/idea`), Discord sends the Bot the information needed to reply to you, such as the command name, the server and channel it was used in, and your Discord user ID. The Bot uses this only to send its reply and does not save it to a database or file.

The Bot does not:

- read or store the content of messages in your server
- track or profile users
- store usernames, user IDs, server IDs, or message history
- share information with third parties

## Logs

The Bot's hosting platform (Heroku) keeps short-term operational logs. These record that a command ran (for example "idea command activated!") and any errors, and are used only to keep the Bot working. They are not used to identify or track individual users.

## Third parties

The Bot runs on Discord and is hosted on Heroku, and use of those services is subject to their own privacy policies:

- Discord: https://discord.com/privacy
- Heroku: https://www.salesforce.com/company/privacy/

## Children

The Bot is subject to Discord's own age requirements and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone.

This policy may be updated from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last changed.
